1. What it is

A system that gathers the public numbers of YouTube and TikTok channels, puts them on a common scale and analyzes them. Use it to compare channels, see how audience is distributed, follow how channels evolve and spot numbers that do not add up.

Besides this guide, every metric has its entry in the metrics manual, which you can also read from the "Manual" tab of the dashboard.

2. Trying it locally

From the project folder, with Python 3.11 or later:

python -m venv .venv
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m pip install -r requirements.txt
.venv/Scripts/python.exe -m uvicorn app.main:app --reload

Then open the dashboard or the API documentation. Data is stored in the channel_analytics.db file.

With or without real data. With no credentials, the system uses simulated data and says so on screen. With YOUTUBE_API_KEY in the .env file it really queries YouTube and spends that key's quota: each topic search costs 100 of the 10,000 free daily units.

3. How it works

How to read the graph

  • Sources and collectors: each collector talks to one platform. With no credentials, it uses simulated data.
  • Normalization: turns every response into one channel format and calculates engagement (NER).
  • Statistics engine and audience signals: analyze those channels. Signals also read the stored history.
  • Session and plans: plans exist, but are only enforced if REQUIRE_SUBSCRIPTION is on. By default, statistics work without logging in.
  • Scheduler and worker: periodically take a snapshot of tracked channels by querying the collectors.

4. What data it handles

The system stores only the channels you choose to track, their periodic snapshots, channel types and user accounts. Searches are not stored. It only works with each channel's public data, not with data about its viewers.

How to read the graph

  • Channel type, Tracked channel and Channel snapshot: each type groups several channels, and each channel keeps at most one snapshot per day.
  • User: only the password's hash is stored. Users are not linked to channels.
  • What is not stored: search results, recent videos and signals are calculated on every query. That is why a tracked channel's signals use its snapshot: they do not include per-video likes, but they do not spend quota either.

5. Audience signals: are the numbers credible?

Buying followers or views leaves traces: many people following a channel and almost nobody watching it. This feature looks for those traces in public numbers. Use it from the "Audiencia" tab of the dashboard.

These are indications, not proof. Public data does not show who is watching or identify bots. A legitimate channel can show these signals too: ask for its internal statistics before concluding anything.

Sudden follower drop5% or more between two snapshots. It may be a purge of fake accounts. Tracked channels only.25

How to read the result

ResultScoreWhat it means
No signalsUnder 25Nothing notable in what could be measured. It does not guarantee the audience is real.
Mild signal25 to 49Something stands out. A single signal never goes beyond this level.
Worth reviewing50 to 74Several signals together: ask for internal data.
Many signals75 or moreSeveral strong signals: do not decide on these numbers alone.
Insufficient data—Fewer than 2 signals could be calculated, or the channel has under 1,000 followers.

The score measures how much a channel is worth reviewing, not the probability of fraud. Signals with no data are listed separately and do not count: hidden likes or disabled comments are not treated as zero.

What to ask a channel for to confirm

Only the channel owner has this data: unique users, watch time, concurrent audience and traffic sources.

From the API

curl "http://localhost:8000/api/v1/analytics/audience-integrity?query=fitness&platform=youtube&limit=30"
curl "http://localhost:8000/api/v1/tracking/channels/1/audience-integrity"

6. Searching and comparing channels

Type a topic at the top of the dashboard and the system fetches its channels:

  • Resumen (summary): channels by size, audience inequality, distributions and correlations.
  • Por canal (by channel): a sortable table, with each channel's details.
  • Por temática (by topic): compares the searches made in the session.
  • Por categoría (by category): a ranking per YouTube category, with no topic to choose.
  • Métricas del medio (media metrics): each platform's reference values.

The API routes are at /docs.

7. Tracking channels

In the "Catálogo" tab you can add channels to tracking and classify them by type. Each one gets a snapshot when added and another on every scheduler run, daily at 09:00 UTC by default. You can also run it by hand with the "Correr job ahora" button. Without that history there are no jump or drop signals: they need at least 4 snapshots.

They are only enforced with REQUIRE_SUBSCRIPTION=true. No real payment is connected: plans are assigned by hand from the "Cuenta" tab.

9. Limitations

  • Thresholds not validated against real fraud: they have not been checked against channels with proven fraud. In a test on 09/11/2026 with 69 real YouTube channels, none reached "worth reviewing". When simulating purchases on 55 of them, 1 in 50 reached "worth reviewing" with 10 times more followers, and half with 20 times more views: it is cautious, and a result with no signals does not rule out a moderate purchase.
  • Public data only: it does not identify bots or individual accounts.
  • No Instagram: only YouTube and TikTok are analyzed today.
  • TikTok: recent videos are not evaluated, and the official API may limit what can be obtained depending on approved access.
  • Small channels: with under 1,000 followers they are barely evaluated, because ratios swing too much.
  • Rounded subscribers: YouTube reports them rounded to 3 figures, so changes under 5% are not evaluated.
